Our Story

A golden opportunity for every Ugandan far from home.

For anyone in the UAE who misses the flavours of home: every week, our company The Pearl of Africa brings fresh produce straight from Uganda. At Oryx Village, our new restaurant, it's cooked right in front of you by experienced hands.

One phone call is all it takes: our riders bring your meal right to your door.

“What makes us special: the food is grown on our own farms and packed in our own kitchen.”— The Pearl of Africa Grocery
